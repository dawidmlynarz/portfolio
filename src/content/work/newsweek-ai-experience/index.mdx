## My role

As Senior Product Designer at Newsweek, I co-led the AI integration design effort alongside fellow designer, Jonathan Mora. My work spanned the full product surface, from the live AI-powered search experience to a suite of in-development features including a fully conversational AI assistant, AI-powered personalised homepage feed, article-level text-to-speech, and AI translation.

I was responsible for the UX strategy, all design concepts presented to stakeholders, and collaborated on the competitive audit that framed the opportunity. The work was built on Newsweek's design system, ensuring every new AI touchpoint remained coherent with the broader product.

The AI concepts were presented by George Yoon, Director of Product Design at the Google AI Impact Summit 2025, attended by all major US publishers, as a way for how to disrupt new media. The outcome was Google funding to build the AI chat experience on Gemini.

### Key achievements

- Google funding secured to build conversational AI experience on Gemini, following presentation at Google AI Impact Summit 2025.
- Competitive audit established that no major publisher had solved native AI integration, framing Newsweek's opportunity clearly for internal and external stakeholders.
- AI roadmap in active development, with AI Search live and the personalised feed and full assistant progressing through engineering.

## Project background

Newsweek has over 90 years of journalism behind it. It's a vast, authoritative archive that most readers access through a search bar or social referral, and then leave. The challenge wasn't a lack of content. It was the interface standing between readers and the full depth of what Newsweek had to offer.

At the same time, the broader media landscape was shifting fast. Competitors were beginning to layer AI into their products, but in fragmented, inconsistent ways e.g. a search summary here, a floating chatbot there. No major publisher had yet made AI feel native to the reading experience.

**The opportunity was clear:** Newsweek, with its depth of archive and global readership, was positioned to be the first legacy publisher to treat AI not as a utility bolt-on, but as the primary interface for journalism.
