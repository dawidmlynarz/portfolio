## My role

As a Lead Product Designer for over a year, **I redesigned strategic parts of the product to improve conversion of the affiliate marketing funnel** while working on new features in parallel, as well as making continuous UX improvements followed by A/B testing.

Some other projects included advanced mortgage calculators, navigation redesign, mortgage rate tables, and increasing consistency between NerdWallet Canada, UK, and US versions.

### Key achievements

- Increased the clickthrough rates (CTRs) of key parts of the product by 80%, positively impacting affiliate income.
- Reduced the bounce rate from 45% to 30% with redesigned navigation.

## Project Background

NerdWallet’s core business is based on affiliate income from financial products offered on their site. **The main goal of the website is to convert visitors to round-up pages** offering credit cards, mortgages, banking, and more financial products. The business goal was to optimize the conversion of the affiliate marketing funnel.

Our product team (from XWP) was responsible for a series of conversion optimization initiatives to keep users engaged with the NerdWallet Canada site. **We conducted a series of workshops with stakeholders** to align on business and user goals.

The main **user goal was to easily discover finance products** they need, compare them, and make a decision to purchase the product that best suits them.

### Objectives

- Conversion optimization of the affiliate marketing funnel
- UX improvements of round-up pages to boost clickthrough rates (CTR)
- Navigation redesign to reduce bounce rate and improve the browsing experience
- Creation of mortgage calculators to increase the amount of time visitors spend on the site, preventing them from leaving for other banking sites

## Low-Hanging Fruits

We regrouped to prioritize the work and divided it into a logical sequence of improvements to ensure the overall UX of the site was not impacted.

**We decided to start with continuous UX improvements through a series of many A/B tests**, each running for a minimum of two weeks (one sprint cycle). These tests involved key parts of the product, such as comparison tables, product cards, filtering, mortgage rate tables, and more.

This initiative **increased the CTRs of key parts of the product by 80%**, positively impacting affiliate income. We tracked the impact of our UX improvements using Google Analytics dashboards and reported the outcomes to the client.

Additionally, this helped us maintain a healthy relationship with external stakeholders and showcase not only the progress we were making but also the real impact on conversion rates.

## Navigation redesign

Next, we focused on the more significant task – navigation redesign. We studied the existing structure of NerdWallet CA to understand its complexity and provided an updated information architecture for the navigation.

**We prioritized the ability to reach the round-up pages as quickly as possible** to present users with the offers they might be interested in, depending on the category of products they were looking for.

We decided to use the mega-menu navigation pattern for desktop and nested navigation for mobile. **Our goal was to maximize the potential of the screen’s real estate and provide the best possible experience**, regardless of the device.

The navigation was introduced in iterations, with each iteration adding a new set of features. We started with a simple navigation refresh, then added the next level of navigation, and eventually introduced the desired solution. We tested the impact on the product at every stage of the process.

Soon after the release, we discovered that the **bounce rate had decreased from 45% to 30%**, a significant improvement. We also noticed an increase in user traffic on round-up pages coming from the navigation itself (organic traffic).

## Research

Before diving into this project, it was crucial to understand the Canadian mortgage market. I invested a significant amount of time in market research. Additionally, we received substantial industry insights from the NerdWallet team.

**To better understand existing market solutions, I conducted a competitive analysis of direct competitors and commercial banks in Canada.** I looked for best practices and areas for improvement when building NerdWallet mortgage calculators.

I found that very few calculators provided the ability to compare more than one offer at a time. Therefore, we decided to introduce this unique feature to our calculators, allowing users to compare two mortgage scenarios simultaneously and highlight the differences between the offers.

## Design

Once the research was done, I focused on designing low-fidelity prototypes of the experience. During the process, I ensured we could provide a responsive solution that works well across all devices.

Due to the large complexity of the calculators, I worked closely with NerdWallet stakeholders to define the minimum amount of data needed from users to lower the learning curve as much as possible.

It was important to display complex data in an accessible way. Therefore, I introduced a pie chart presenting a clear breakdown of the estimated monthly payment. Transparency of the overall costs of the mortgage was crucial to make users feel comfortable deciding on the offer.

## Usability testing

After several iterations, the approach was presented to the client, and we decided to conduct usability testing of the high-fidelity version. **We ran an unmoderated test with 15 participants, 47% of whom had used mortgage calculators before.**

Users were asked several closed and open questions to provide further context on their experience with finances. They completed unmoderated tasks on the interactive Figma prototype set up with [Maze tool](https://maze.co/).

We wanted to ensure users understood the calculator and that the comparison feature did not impose excessive complexity. Based on the insights collected from user testing, I prepared a presentation for the stakeholders showing the results and UX improvements for the next iteration.

Once the latest iteration of designs was ready, I worked closely with engineers to hand over the project and conducted several design QA sessions to ensure all interactions were implemented properly.

## Key Takeaways

We were proud to **increase the CTRs of key parts of the product by 80%**, positively impacting affiliate income, and **reducing the bounce rate from 45% to 30%** with redesigned navigation.

Additionally, we delivered complex mortgage calculators in a user-friendly format. Usability testing was crucial – not only for understanding the user’s perspective but also for properly scoping the work given the client’s budget and timeline.

My key takeaway from this project was that when working on complex solutions, it is essential to deliver everything in smaller iterations to show progress more often and lower the risk of impacting the overall UX of the product.
