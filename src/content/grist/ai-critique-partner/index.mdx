Critique can be really useful and is often wasted. This skill covers both ends, so you go into the review prepared and come out with decisions written down.

Don't skip the real review because the AI one went well. This gets you ready for your colleagues. They still need to see it.

## Example

Before a review of a new onboarding flow, the skill's prep sheet lists one gap (no stated constraint on legal copy) and its strongest objection, _"Skipping account setup until later moves the hard step to the moment people are trying to use the product."_ The designer adds a slide on exactly that.
