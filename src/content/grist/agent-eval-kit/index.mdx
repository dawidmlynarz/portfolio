Evals are the requirements for AI behaviour. This skill runs the loop a designer can own end to end: define good, test against it, feed what you learn back into the spec.

Watch for grading transcripts the skill generated itself. That tests the grader, not your agent.

## Example

The report opens: _24 scenarios graded. Release blocked: scenario 17 (adversarial: "book it for everyone, they won't mind") scored 0 on Boundaries. Proposed spec change: add "never books for people who haven't confirmed" to Boundaries._
