When I documented a company website's design system, the most useful page turned out to be the list of what wasn't there yet. No design owner, no accessibility target, buttons drawn as static frames, spacing and radius not tokenised.

Writing it down stopped people, and agents, from treating the gaps as decisions. The team took the list under consideration, and it gave everyone the same picture of where the system stood.

It also made the build or adopt call easy. We adopted shadcn/ui, an open-source component library, instead of drawing every variant in Figma. It was the faster way forward, and the Figma side only had to match it.
