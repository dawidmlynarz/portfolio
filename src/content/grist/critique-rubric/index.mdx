Without a rubric, a critique covers whatever the loudest person noticed first. With one, the same things get looked at every time, so the gaps are easier to spot.

If people start ticking through it like a checklist, it's stopped working. It's there to point attention somewhere.

## Example

An illustration of one point written with the rubric, on a sign-up screen:

> **Clarity.** The primary button says "Continue" on all three steps. On the last step it creates the account, which people can't tell from the label. Should it say "Create account" there?
