Critique is the designer's sharpest tool and the one most often wasted. This skill runs the whole loop, so you walk into every review prepared and walk out with decisions.

Watch for skipping the real critique because the AI one went well. This prepares you for colleagues; it doesn't replace them.

## Example

Before a review of a new onboarding flow, the skill's prep sheet lists one gap (no stated constraint on legal copy) and its strongest objection: _"Skipping account setup until later moves the hard step to the moment people are trying to use the product."_ The designer adds a slide on exactly that.
