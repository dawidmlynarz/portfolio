I ran workshops where a company works out where AI fits, and introduced them as a practice at Zazmic, the consultancy I work with. I bring a deliberately concrete first answer, what consultants call a straw man proposal: the current state, the opportunities and a rough roadmap. When it was vague, people nodded along. When it named systems, numbers and owners, they corrected it.

The corrections were the useful part.
