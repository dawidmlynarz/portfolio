Designers draw the happy path. Agents go wrong off it. They do the wrong thing with confidence, do too much, or get stuck and say nothing. If you list those early, they become requirements. Later they're incidents.

"The AI might hallucinate" isn't a failure you can design for. Push until each one names a specific action of yours.
