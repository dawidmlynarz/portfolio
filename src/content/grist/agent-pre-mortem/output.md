Agent: books group travel for a team of 6 to 12
Actions: search flights, hold seats, book, email the team

Can't be undone

1. Wrong action. It books the 7:05 flight when the lead said "morning", and two people can't make it.
   Likelihood: medium · Undo: no (non-refundable fare)
   Response: checkpoint before booking that lists each person, time and fare rule.

2. Overreach. It emails the whole team the itinerary before the lead has seen it.
   Likelihood: medium · Undo: no
   Response: emails go out only after the lead approves the draft.

Can be undone with effort 3. Stall. Seats on hold expire while it waits for one person's passport details, and it says nothing.
Likelihood: high · Undo: with effort
Response: show the hold's expiry time, and ask the lead once it's 2 hours away.

Can be undone 4. Misleading. It says "all booked" when one ticket failed at payment.
Likelihood: low · Undo: yes
Response: a per-person status list, with failures at the top.
