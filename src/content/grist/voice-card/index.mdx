An agent's voice is written by many hands: designers, prompt writers, engineers fixing edge cases. Without a card, it drifts message by message until it sounds like nobody in particular.

Watch for a voice that's charming in success and useless in failure. Test the card against the error and refusal lines first.

## Example

The _Hard moments_ section for a booking agent: _When it can't do something: "I can't book trains yet. Here's the operator's page with your dates filled in." When it made a mistake: "I held the wrong date. I've released it and held the 14th instead. Nothing was charged."_
