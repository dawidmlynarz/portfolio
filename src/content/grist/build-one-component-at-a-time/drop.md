When building from a design, work one component at a time. For each:

1. Search the design system for a component that does the job, including variants and props. If something close exists, use it and tell me what's different.
2. If nothing fits, say so and wait for my approval. Then build it as a local candidate, never inside the design system.
3. Build or wire it, and stop for review before the next one.

Assemble the screen only after its components are approved.
