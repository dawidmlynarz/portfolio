Verdict
Build discovery as an assistant. Let the agent act only to hold seats. Purchases stay with the person.

Top five issues

1. High. The purchase is step 3 of a 4-step plan that's approved as a whole ("Approve plan").
   Fix: a separate checkpoint right before payment, with the total and the refund rule.
2. High. It can pay with the saved card without saying which one.
   Fix: name the card and the amount in the checkpoint.
3. Medium. A hold that expires is silent. The person finds out at checkout.
   Fix: show the expiry on the plan and warn 10 minutes before.
4. Medium. It asks to confirm the seat map view and the date filter.
   Fix: remove both checkpoints. They can be undone in one click.
5. Low. "Done!" after holding seats reads like a purchase.
   Fix: "Seats held until 18:40. Not paid yet."
