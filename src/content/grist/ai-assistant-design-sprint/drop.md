# AI assistant design sprint

## 1. Competitive audit

For each assistant: where it lives, what it can do (answer, recommend, act), how it hands off to a person, how it handles errors, its tone. [Add your criteria]

## 2. Journey map

The stages the assistant supports, from the first question to after the booking. For each: what the person wants, what the assistant does, what could go wrong.

## 3. Interaction spec

Per stage: what the assistant says, what it shows (cards, options, summaries), what it does, and what it never does.

## 4. Scripted prototype

A clickable prototype of the main flow with real-looking content and fixed responses. No model yet.

## 5. Review

Walk the client through the prototype. Write down every decision and every open question.

## Output

Audit table · Journey map · Interaction spec · Prototype link · Decisions
