Agents get it wrong both ways. People ignore them when they're right and follow them when they're wrong. What you want is trust that matches how reliable the agent really is in that moment, which sometimes means less of it.

Confidence percentages look like a fix but people misread them. Plain categories, or showing alternatives, often work better. Test whichever you pick.
