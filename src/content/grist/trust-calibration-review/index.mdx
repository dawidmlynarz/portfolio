Agents fail at both ends: ignored when they're right, followed when they're wrong. The goal isn't more trust; it's trust that matches how reliable the agent actually is in each situation.

Watch for adding confidence percentages as a fix. Numbers are easy to misread; categories or alternatives often work better, and should be tested.
