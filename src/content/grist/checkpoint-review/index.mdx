Most agent flows fail checkpoints in the same three ways: they pause on harmless steps, skip the one irreversible step, or hide it inside a plan people approve in one click.

Watch for reviewing the screens without the actions behind them. A checkpoint can look fine and still be in the wrong place.

## Example

A ticket-buying assistant asks "Here's my plan: find shows, pick seats, buy tickets, email confirmations. Looks good?" It fails coverage (the purchase is buried in step 3) and content (no price, no refund policy). The fix: the plan runs without approval, then a separate checkpoint: _Buy 2 tickets · €184 including fees · not refundable_ with Approve, Change seats and Stop, and the line _No answer in 10 minutes? Nothing gets bought._
