## SOW review for designers

### Implied work

- [ ] Research, content, accessibility and design system work are listed if the deliverables need them
- [ ] Review rounds are stated: how many, and who reviews

### Deliverables

- [ ] Each deliverable says what done looks like
- [ ] Fidelity is stated (sketch, wireframe, high fidelity, prototype)
- [ ] Formats are stated (Figma, code, document)

### Dependencies

- [ ] Client inputs are listed with dates: content, brand assets, access, approvals
- [ ] Engineering dependencies are named

### Risk

- [ ] Assumptions that could blow the estimate are written down
- [ ] Exclusions are explicit

### Language

- [ ] No words nobody can sign off on ("best-in-class", "seamless", "intuitive")
