Any tool works for the map. I first built it in FigJam, because the board was the base for a workshop with the client afterwards. It works just as well as a table in a doc, or as an artifact your AI tool draws from your notes. I reused the same structure on a second project on purpose. Reusing it saved the setup and made the two easy to compare.

It has two layers. The first is a journey table, with the goal, mood, actions and friction at each stage. The second sits right under it and shows what an agent would do at the same stage, and what stays with the person. Next to it I compared where the agent should live on the page.

## Example

Here's an illustration for a weekly grocery order. At the basket stage people are strained, because rebuilding last week's order takes ages. The agent's role is to rebuild it from past orders and flag what's out of stock. The person's role is to swap items and confirm. Leverage is highest here, and nothing is paid until the person checks out, so it goes into the MVP.
