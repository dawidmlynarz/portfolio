Lots of people end up writing an agent's lines. Designers, whoever writes the prompt, an engineer patching an edge case at 6pm. Without a card the voice drifts a little with each message, and after a while it sounds like nobody.

Plenty of voices are charming when things work and useless when they don't. Try the card on the error and refusal lines first.

## Example

Here's the _Hard moments_ section for a booking agent. _When it can't do something: "I can't book trains yet. Here's the operator's page with your dates filled in." When it made a mistake: "I held the wrong date. I've released it and held the 14th instead. Nothing was charged."_
