This season's drops are the handoff, done properly. As a skill, the coding agent writes its own brief with you, builds against it, and stops for your review.

Watch for skipping the brief because the design "is clear". The brief is where intent and states get written down.

## Example

At the end, the skill reports: _Built 6 components (5 from the system, 1 local candidate: "seat-map-legend", awaiting your approval). 14 of 15 acceptance criteria pass. Failing: long venue names overflow on small screens. Fix now?_
