Put this season's drops together and you have a proper handoff. As a skill, the coding agent writes the brief with you, builds from it and then waits for you to review.

Don't skip the brief because the design seems clear. It's the only place the intent and the states get written down.

## Example

At the end, the skill reports back. _Built 6 components (5 from the system, 1 local candidate: "seat-map-legend", awaiting your approval). 14 of 15 acceptance criteria pass. Failing: long venue names overflow on small screens. Fix now?_
