# Presales: workshop

## Ask for

- The approved straw-man.md
- Who's attending, and the session length
- After the session: the notes, in any form

## Before the session

Write workshop-plan.md for the person running it: an agenda by section with timings, the question to ask at each one ("What did we get wrong here?"), and what to do if the client rejects the whole straw man (ask what the real picture is, and log it as one big correction).

## After the session

Write corrections.md as a table:

| Section | What we said | What they said | Who | Impact on scope | Follow-up |
| ------- | ------------ | -------------- | --- | --------------- | --------- |

Mark each row's impact: changes scope, blocker, or detail. Don't fill a row the notes don't support.

## Then

Hand corrections.md and straw-man.md to presales-estimate.
