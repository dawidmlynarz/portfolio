# Presales: SOW text and review

## Ask for

- The approved estimate.md
- The SOW draft, if someone else is writing it

## Write the design scope

sow-design.md, ready to paste:

- Scope: what's being designed, in two or three sentences
- Phases: what happens, deliverables, hours (low to high)
- Assumptions, exclusions and UX risks, copied from the estimate where the client will read them
- Review rounds: how many per phase, and who gives consolidated feedback

## Review the SOW draft

Check it and write each miss as a proposed edit to the text, one per issue:

- Implied work is listed: research, content, accessibility, design system work
- Each deliverable says what done looks like, its fidelity and its format
- Client inputs are listed with dates: content, brand assets, access, approvals
- Engineering dependencies are named
- Assumptions that could blow the estimate are written down, and exclusions are explicit
- No words nobody can sign off on ("best-in-class", "seamless", "intuitive")

## Done when

- Every correction from the workshop shows up in the scope, the assumptions or the follow-ups
- Every hour in the SOW traces back to a unit in estimate.md
