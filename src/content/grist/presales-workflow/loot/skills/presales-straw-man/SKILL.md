# Presales: straw man

## Ask for

- Client: industry, size, what they sell
- Notes from early calls, and anything the client has shared
- What the workshop needs the client to decide

## Do

Draft straw-man.md with three sections:

1. Current state: how the work is done today, the systems involved, where it's slow or costly.
2. Opportunities: five to eight places AI could help, each with who benefits and what would change.
3. First roadmap: three phases, each with a goal and a rough length.

Be specific enough to be wrong: name systems, teams and numbers from the notes. Mark every claim (from notes), (assumption) or (guess). End with the five questions most likely to change the roadmap.

## Check before handing on

- Every section names real systems or teams
- No section is only guesses
- Nothing in it you wouldn't say out loud in the room

## Then

Stop. Show the straw man and ask for approval (checkpoint 1). After approval, hand straw-man.md to presales-workshop.
