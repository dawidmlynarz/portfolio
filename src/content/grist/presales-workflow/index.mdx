This is how I run presales on AI projects, end to end. Each drop in this season is one part of it. Here they hand off to each other: the straw man feeds the workshop, the workshop's corrections feed the estimate, and the estimate becomes the SOW text.

I stop it twice. Once before the straw man goes in front of a client, and once before any hours go into a SOW. A separate reviewer agent reads every estimate looking for missing assumptions and exclusions, because that's where estimates go wrong later. [YOU: the step you'd never skip, and why.]
