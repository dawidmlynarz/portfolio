The agent writes the brief, so the customer doesn't have to. When someone asks for a human they've usually explained the problem once already. Make them do it again and you've probably lost them.

The brief can be short. Say who this is, what they want, what the agent already tried and why it’s handing over. The person picking it up should get it in ten seconds.
