The agent writes the brief, not the customer. By the time someone asks for a human they have usually explained the problem once already. Asking them to explain it again is the quickest way to lose them.

The brief can be short: who this is, what they want, what the agent already tried and why it’s handing over. The person picking it up should get it in ten seconds.
