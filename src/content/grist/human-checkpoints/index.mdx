Checkpoints should follow what an action costs, not how confident the model is. Pause when an action can’t be undone, spends money, or speaks for the person. For everything else, let the agent act, keep a readable log, and make undo easy.

The common failure isn’t too few checkpoints. It’s too many. When every step asks for approval, people stop reading and start clicking, and the one pause that mattered gets the same glance as the nine that didn’t.
